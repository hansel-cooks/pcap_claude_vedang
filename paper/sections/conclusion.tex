\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We parallelised a complete CLAHE--Frangi--morphology retinal vessel pipeline with OpenMP and measured it stage by
stage against a pragma-free serial build of the same C code. The parallel version is bit-identical to the
serial one and reaches 2.08$\times$ on two physical cores with Hyper-Threading, or 19.2 DRIVE images per second.
Its accuracy (Dice 0.713 on DRIVE and 0.654 on STARE) is below that of lightweight deep models, but needs no
training. The Gaussian-derivative passes dominate the cost. The design decisions that matter most are thread
placement on SMT cores, owner-computes histograms, and parallelism inside each Frangi scale. Future work covers
machines with more cores, cache blocking of the column passes, fusing the small stages into one parallel
region, and the GPU comparison planned in the original proposal.
